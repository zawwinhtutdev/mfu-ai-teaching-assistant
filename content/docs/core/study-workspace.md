The MFU AI study workspace groups four course-aware tools: **AI Chat**, **Flashcards**, **Quizzes**, and **Planner**. Open **AI Study Tools**, select a course, and then choose the tool that matches your learning goal.

## Choose the right tool

| Goal | Tool | Best use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Understand a concept | AI Chat | Explanations, examples, and guided questions. |
| Recall terms or steps | Flashcards | Repeated question-and-answer review. |
| Check understanding | Quizzes | Complete questions before seeing your score. |
| Organize revision | Planner | Prioritized manual or AI-generated study tasks. |

## A useful study cycle

1. **Orient:** read the learning objective and relevant course material.
2. **Clarify:** ask AI Chat about one difficult point.
3. **Recall:** answer flashcards before flipping them.
4. **Test:** complete a quiz without looking at the source.
5. **Plan:** turn weak areas into study tasks.
6. **Verify:** return to the course document or teacher guidance for anything uncertain.

This cycle is more effective than repeatedly requesting summaries because it requires you to retrieve and apply information.

## Start from the correct course

Course selection controls the context and available activities. Check the course code and title before beginning. If a course is missing, verify enrollment from **My Courses** or contact the teacher.

## When a tool is empty

An empty flashcard or quiz area usually means no activity is available for the selected course or account. It does not mean the entire study workspace is unavailable. Continue with AI Chat or Planner and ask the teacher whether practice material will be added.

## Keep AI support productive

- Attempt a question before asking for the solution.
- Ask for hints or a checking question when you are learning a method.
- Record which topics remain difficult.
- Do not treat a generated score or explanation as an official grade.
