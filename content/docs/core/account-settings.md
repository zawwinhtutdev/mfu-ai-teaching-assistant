---
title: Account and settings
description: Review your profile, display preferences, account access, and privacy-related options.
---

Open **Settings** from the student or teacher portal. The exact controls shown depend on your account role and the current platform configuration.

## Review your profile

Check that your display name and university account information are accurate. Your profile may be used to identify you in courses, support requests, and administrative workflows.

## Change the display theme

Use the theme control to switch between light and dark mode. The preference is stored in the browser, so another browser or device may use a different setting.

## Protect your session

- Use a unique password.
- Do not share authentication links or browser sessions.
- Sign out when using a shared computer.
- Reset your password if you believe the account may be compromised.

## Review privacy choices

Read any consent or privacy information shown by the platform before enabling optional processing. Ask an administrator when you are unsure how a setting affects stored data.

## Sign out

Use **Sign out** from the account menu or portal navigation. Closing the browser window alone may not end the session.
