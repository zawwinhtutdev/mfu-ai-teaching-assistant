The **MFU Campus Guide** provides convenient links to commonly used university resources. Students can also open **MFU Social** to discover selected university communities and activities.

## Open a campus resource

1. Select **MFU Campus Guide** in the student or teacher portal.
2. Find the service you need.
3. Open the resource in its official website.
4. Confirm important information on that official page.

Available links may include student systems, learning services, university information, employment resources, facilities, and other campus services.

## Explore student communities

Students can use **MFU Social** to find selected clubs, representative organizations, library channels, and activity information.

## Verify external information

Campus resources are maintained by different university units. Their availability, policies, and contact information can change independently of MFU AI.

> Treat the linked university website—not a summary generated by AI—as the final source for fees, admissions, academic rules, dates, and official announcements.
