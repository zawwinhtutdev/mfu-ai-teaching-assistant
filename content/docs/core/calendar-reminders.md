---
title: Calendar and reminders
description: Organize course events, personal study tasks, and upcoming work from your portal.
---

Students and teachers can open **Calendar** from their portal sidebar. Students also have a dedicated **Reminders** area for personal study planning.

## Review the calendar

Use the calendar to move between dates and review the events available to your account. Course schedules and personal planning items may appear together, depending on the current configuration.

1. Open **Calendar** from the portal.
2. Move to the required day, week, or month.
3. Select an event to review its available details.
4. Compare assessment dates with the official course announcement or syllabus.

## Create useful reminders

A useful reminder describes one action and includes enough context to complete it.

```text
Review Week 6 regression examples before the tutorial
```

Avoid vague reminders such as “study more.” Split a large task into smaller actions that can be completed in one session.

## Manage completed work

Mark a reminder complete when the action is finished. Remove obsolete reminders so the list continues to represent current work.

> Calendar and reminder tools help with planning, but official course instructions remain the authoritative source for deadlines.
