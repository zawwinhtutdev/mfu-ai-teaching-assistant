---
title: Users and roles
description: Review accounts and apply student, teacher, and administrator access with appropriate care.
---

Open **Users** from the admin console to review the accounts available to the platform.

## Understand platform roles

- **Student** access supports courses, chat, study tools, documents, reminders, and student resources.
- **Teacher** access supports course management, assistant configuration, documents, and analytics.
- **Administrator** access supports user management, audit records, privacy, notifications, and policies.

## Change a role safely

1. Verify the person’s identity and authorization.
2. Review their current role.
3. Apply the least privilege necessary.
4. Save the change.
5. Confirm the expected portal opens and restricted routes remain unavailable.

## Resolve incorrect access

Do not create duplicate accounts as a first response. Check the account email, stored role, enrollment, and active session. Ask the user to sign out and sign in again after an authorized role change.

## Protect administrative access

Administrator privileges should be rare, reviewed periodically, and removed promptly when no longer required.
