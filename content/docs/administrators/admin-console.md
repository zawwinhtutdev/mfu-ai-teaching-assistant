---
title: Admin console
description: Operate user management, policies, privacy controls, notifications, and audit records.
---

The admin console is separate from student and teacher portals. Access should be limited to authorized administrators.

## Console areas

| Area | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | Review the current operational summary. |
| Users | Inspect and manage user accounts and roles. |
| Audit Logs | Review recorded administrative and platform events. |
| Privacy | Manage privacy-related controls and information. |
| Notifications | Review or operate notification processes. |
| Policies | Configure supported platform policy values. |

## Manage roles carefully

Role changes affect access to student, teacher, and administrative features. Verify the user’s identity and authorization before changing a role. Apply the least privilege needed for the person’s responsibilities.

## Use audit records

Audit logs support operational review and investigation. They should be protected from unnecessary access and retained according to university policy.

## Change policies safely

Understand the effect of a policy before changing it. Record the reason, owner, and expected outcome for significant changes, then verify the affected workflow after saving.

> Administrator documentation intentionally avoids publishing credentials, secrets, or internal security procedures. Store those materials in an approved restricted system.
