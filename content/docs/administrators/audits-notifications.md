---
title: Audits and notifications
description: Use audit records for operational review and monitor notification-related activity.
---

The admin console provides **Audit Logs** and **Notifications** areas for authorized operational work.

## Review audit records

Audit records may include the actor, action, resource, time, and supporting metadata associated with an event. Use filters or identifiers to narrow an investigation rather than exporting unrelated records.

## Interpret events carefully

An audit entry confirms that an event was recorded; it may not contain the complete reason or user intent. Compare it with the affected resource and other authorized operational evidence.

## Monitor notifications

Use the notifications area to review the state exposed by the current platform. When a delivery problem is reported, confirm the recipient, event time, notification type, and whether the underlying job completed.

## Protect operational data

Audit and notification information may contain personal or security-relevant metadata. Share it only with authorized people and retain it according to university policy.
