---
title: Privacy and security
description: Apply privacy controls, protect sensitive information, and review platform policy settings.
---

Privacy and security are shared responsibilities across administrators, teachers, students, and system maintainers.

## Minimize collected data

Collect and retain only the information required for an approved educational or operational purpose. Avoid copying sensitive content into support tickets, chat prompts, or analytics exports.

## Review privacy controls

Use the **Privacy** and **Policies** areas of the admin console to review the supported controls. Understand the affected workflow before changing a value.

## Protect secrets

API keys, database credentials, service tokens, and administrator passwords do not belong in public documentation, source screenshots, or ordinary chat messages. Store them in the approved secret-management system.

## Respond to concerns

When a privacy or security issue is reported:

1. Preserve the minimum evidence needed for investigation.
2. Limit further exposure.
3. Notify the responsible university or project owner.
4. Follow the approved incident and privacy process.

> This public guide provides operational principles, not confidential security procedures.
