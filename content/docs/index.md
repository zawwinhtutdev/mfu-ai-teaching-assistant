## The MFU learning loop

1. **Choose context** — select the course or indexed document that should guide the answer.
2. **Ask with intent** — name the learning goal and the format you need.
3. **Practice** — use flashcards, quizzes, or a checking question instead of only reading.
4. **Verify** — compare important claims with the original material or instructor guidance.
5. **Plan the next action** — turn uncertainty into a concrete study or teaching task.

## Browse by role

Use the audience tabs in the left sidebar:

- **Explore** shows shared platform and AI-literacy guides.

- **Teach** adds course delivery and assistant configuration.
- **Admin** focuses on role, privacy, policy, notification, and audit operations.

## Before you begin

You need an MFU university email address and a modern web browser. The latest versions of Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge are recommended.

1. [Create your account](/docs/getting-started/create-account).
2. [Sign in](/docs/getting-started/sign-in).
3. Choose **Learn**, **Teach**, or **Admin** in this field guide.
4. Follow the shortest workflow that matches your goal.
