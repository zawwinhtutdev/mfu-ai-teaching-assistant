---
title: Responsible AI use
description: Use MFU AI to strengthen learning while respecting academic integrity, privacy, and human responsibility.
---

MFU AI is designed to assist people, not replace academic judgment or responsibility. Course rules determine which uses are permitted for each learning activity.

## Support learning rather than bypass it

Appropriate uses may include requesting explanations, generating practice questions, comparing approaches, planning revision, and receiving feedback on reasoning.

Do not use AI to impersonate your own work, fabricate evidence, or complete a restricted assessment.

## Follow course instructions

Teachers may allow, limit, or prohibit AI for particular tasks. Ask when the rule is unclear and disclose AI assistance when required.

## Protect people and information

Do not enter passwords, identity documents, grades, private student records, health data, or confidential university material into a prompt.

## Keep a human accountable

Students remain responsible for submitted work. Teachers remain responsible for instructional and assessment decisions. Administrators remain responsible for access, policy, and operational actions.

## Report harmful or unreliable behavior

Save the relevant non-sensitive details, describe what happened, and contact the project team. Do not redistribute harmful output unnecessarily.
