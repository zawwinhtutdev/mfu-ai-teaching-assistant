---
title: Evaluate AI answers
description: Check generated responses for relevance, accuracy, evidence, uncertainty, and appropriate scope.
---

AI-generated text can sound confident even when it is incomplete. Evaluate the response before relying on it in study, teaching, or administration.

## Use the RECAP check

| Check | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Relevance | Does the answer address the question actually asked? |
| Evidence | Can important claims be matched to course material or an official source? |
| Consistency | Does it agree with definitions and rules used in the course? |
| Accuracy | Are facts, calculations, quotations, and citations correct? |
| Purpose | Is the answer appropriate for the learning task and academic-integrity rules? |

## Test calculations and citations

Repeat calculations independently and open cited sources. Never assume a reference exists merely because it is formatted convincingly.

## Look for uncertainty

A responsible answer should distinguish known information from assumptions. Ask “Which parts of this answer are uncertain?” or “What source supports each important claim?”

## Escalate high-impact questions

Confirm information about grades, deadlines, health, safety, legal matters, university policy, and personal data with the responsible human or official office.
