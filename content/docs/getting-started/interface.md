The exact navigation changes with your role, but the platform follows a consistent structure.

## Main navigation

The portal sidebar contains the tools available to your account. On a smaller screen, use the menu button to open or close it.

- Students see courses, calendar, reminders, AI study tools, documents, campus resources, and settings.
- Teachers see courses, calendar, analytics, agent builder, documents, campus resources, and settings.
- Administrators see operational areas such as users, audit logs, privacy, notifications, and policies.

## Theme and display

Use the theme button to switch between light and dark mode. The setting is stored in your browser and reused during later visits.

## Account settings

Open **Settings** from your portal to review the account options available to your role. Keep your profile information accurate because names and roles may appear in course workflows.

## Documentation navigation

This documentation has three navigation areas:

1. The left sidebar lists every guide.
2. Search at the top finds pages by title, description, or topic. Press `Command + K` on macOS or `Ctrl + K` on Windows.
3. **On this page** links jump to sections in the current guide.
