## Sign in to the platform

1. Open the [login page](/login).
2. Enter your MFU email address.
3. Enter your password.
4. Select **Sign in**.

After authentication, the platform uses your account role to give you access to the appropriate features.

## Reset a forgotten password

1. Select **Forgot password?** from the login page.
2. Enter your MFU email address on the [password reset page](/forgot-password).
3. Open the reset message sent to your inbox.
4. Choose a new, unique password.

Check your spam or junk folder if the message does not arrive after a few minutes.

## Sign out safely

Use the account menu or portal sign-out button when you finish, especially on a shared computer. Signing out closes the active session on that browser.

## Access problems

If your credentials are correct but the wrong portal opens, do not create another account. Contact an administrator so the role attached to your profile can be reviewed.
