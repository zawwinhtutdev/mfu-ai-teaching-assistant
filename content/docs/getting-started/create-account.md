Accounts connect you to the correct MFU AI portal and protect course content from unauthorized access.

## Register with your MFU email

1. Open the [sign-up page](/signup).
2. Enter your name and official university email address.
3. Create a strong password that you do not use on another service.
4. Select the requested role if the registration form asks for it.
5. Submit the form and follow any verification instructions sent to your email.

Accepted accounts use an approved MFU email domain, such as `@mfu.ac.th` or `@lamduan.mfu.ac.th`.

## Choose the correct role

Your role determines which portal and features you can access.

- **Student:** course participation, study tools, documents, reminders, and AI chat.
- **Teacher:** course management, document uploads, assistant configuration, and analytics.
- **Administrator:** platform operations and policy controls. Administrator access may require separate approval.

## Protect your account

- Use a unique password.
- Do not share your sign-in details or verification links.
- Sign out on shared devices.
- Review unexpected account activity with the project team.

## If registration fails

Check that the email address is typed correctly and belongs to an accepted MFU domain. If the account already exists, go to [Sign in](/docs/getting-started/sign-in) or use the password-reset option.
