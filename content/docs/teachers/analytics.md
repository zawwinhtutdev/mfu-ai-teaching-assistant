---
title: Analytics
description: Interpret course and assistant activity without treating usage metrics as a substitute for academic judgment.
---

Open [Analytics](/teacherportal/analytics) from the teacher portal. The dashboard presents the activity data currently available for your courses.

## Review activity patterns

Analytics may help you identify:

- Courses or periods with higher activity.
- Topics that generate repeated questions.
- Changes in use around assessments or deadlines.
- Areas where documentation or assistant instructions may need improvement.

## Use metrics responsibly

Activity is not the same as understanding. A low number of questions does not prove that students understand a topic, and a high number does not prove poor performance.

Use analytics as one signal alongside class interaction, assessment evidence, student feedback, and professional judgment.

## Protect student privacy

Access analytics only for legitimate educational purposes. Do not export or share identifiable activity unless that use is authorized by university policy and communicated appropriately.
