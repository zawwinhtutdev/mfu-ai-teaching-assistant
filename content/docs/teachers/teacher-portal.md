The teacher portal groups the tools used to prepare and support courses. Open the [teacher portal](/teacherportal) after signing in.

![Teacher portal showing course management and teaching tools](/docs/teacher-portal.png)

## Manage your courses

Use **My courses** to open an existing course or create course content where that option is available. Course pages are the main place for organizing learner-facing information and tools.

Before making material available, check the course title, code, description, and intended audience.

## Configure AI support

Use **Agent Builder** to define how a course assistant should behave. Clear instructions, well-organized documents, and realistic test questions improve the quality of responses.

## Work with course documents

Use **Documents RAG** to upload and manage material used for document-grounded answers. Remove outdated copies and use recognizable filenames so sources remain understandable.

## Review activity

Use **Analytics** to review the activity signals currently collected for your courses. Analytics can help identify common questions or content gaps, but should not be treated as a complete measure of an individual student’s learning.

## Maintain academic oversight

Review AI-generated content before using it in an assessed or high-impact context. Tell students how AI tools may be used in the course and which academic-integrity rules apply.
