Open **AI Study Tools** from the student portal. The page first shows the available tools, then asks you to select one of your enrolled courses. The course workspace opens with AI Chat, Flashcards, Quizzes, and Planner.

![AI Study Tools course selector](/docs/study-tools.png)

## Open a course tool

1. Select **AI Study Tools** in the student sidebar.
2. Check the four tool cards at the top of the page.
3. Under **Your Courses**, select the course you want to study.
4. Open **AI Assistant** in the course workspace.
5. Choose **AI Chat**, **Flashcards**, **Quizzes**, or **Planner**.

If no courses are listed, enroll in a course or ask the teacher to verify your access.

## Choose a study goal

Start by deciding what you need to accomplish:

- Understand a difficult concept.
- Condense a topic into revision notes.
- Practice retrieving information with flashcards.
- Test understanding with questions.
- Create a short study plan before an assessment.

## Generate useful material

Select the relevant course or source material when that option is available. Describe the difficulty level, scope, and output you want.

```text
Create 10 short-answer questions from Topics 2–4. Mix definitions with application questions and put the answer key at the end.
```

## Study actively

Generated material is a starting point, not the entire study process. Try answering before revealing solutions, explain concepts in your own words, and return to the original course material when something appears inconsistent.

## Keep the scope manageable

Smaller requests usually produce better results. Work with one lecture, topic, or learning objective at a time instead of asking for an entire course in one response.
