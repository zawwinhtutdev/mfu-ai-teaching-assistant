---
title: Study with flashcards
description: Open a course flashcard set, retrieve the answer from memory, reveal it, and move through the set.
---

Flashcards are available inside a course’s **AI Assistant** area. Each card shows a question on the front and an answer on the back.

![Course flashcard question view](/docs/flashcards.png)

## Open the cards

1. Open **AI Study Tools** or **My Courses**.
2. Select the correct course.
3. Open **AI Assistant**.
4. Choose **Flashcards**.

The counter shows your position, for example **Card 2 of 12**. If no cards appear, none are currently available for this course and account.

## Review one card

1. Read the **Question** side.
2. Say or write your answer before revealing anything.
3. Select the card or **Flip Card** to display the **Answer**.
4. Compare the meaning, not only the exact wording.
5. Use **Next** or **Previous** to move through the set.

Moving to another card returns it to the question side.

## Use active recall

Avoid flipping immediately. Give yourself enough time to retrieve the idea, formula, sequence, or definition. When an answer is partly correct, identify the missing element before continuing.

```text
I missed the difference between correlation and causation. Explain the distinction in two sentences, then ask me for a new example.
```

## Build a review cycle

- First pass: answer every card without notes.
- Second pass: revisit the cards you could not explain clearly.
- Final pass: connect two related cards in your own words.

Flashcards support recall but do not replace problem solving, source reading, or instructor feedback.

## No flashcards yet

The empty state means the selected course has no flashcards available to you. Confirm the course selection, then ask your teacher whether a set will be added. You can still use AI Chat to request temporary self-check questions.
