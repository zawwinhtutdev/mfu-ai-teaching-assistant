---
title: Take a practice quiz
description: Select a course quiz, answer every question, submit it, and use the result to guide revision.
---

Course quizzes are formative practice. A result helps you find topics to review; it is not an official course grade unless your teacher explicitly says otherwise.

![Available quizzes in a course](/docs/quizzes.png)

## Start a quiz

1. Open a course from **My Courses** or **AI Study Tools**.
2. Choose **AI Assistant**, then **Quizzes**.
3. Review the quiz title and number of questions.
4. Select **Start Quiz**.

## Answer and submit

Choose one option for every question. The **Submit Quiz** button remains unavailable until all questions have an answer.

Before submitting:

- answer from memory where possible;
- do not use AI Chat to reveal the answer during the attempt;
- note questions that felt uncertain, even if your choice may be correct.

## Read the result

After submission, the page displays a percentage score. Correct answers are marked in green and incorrect selections in red. A score of 70% or above shows the positive completion state; lower scores prompt continued practice.

The color and score summarize this attempt. They do not prove mastery of the full topic.

## Turn mistakes into study actions

1. Write the topic behind each missed question.
2. Return to the relevant course material.
3. Ask for an explanation or a new example—not just the correct letter.
4. Add a task to [Study Planner](/docs/students/study-planner).
5. Try the quiz again only after reviewing.

## Leave the quiz

Use **Back to Quizzes** to return to the list. Starting another quiz clears the answers and result from the current on-screen attempt.

## No quizzes available

The teacher has not made a quiz available for the selected course. Confirm the course and return later, or use AI Chat to create ungraded practice questions from material you are permitted to use.
