---
title: Chat with the AI assistant
description: Ask focused questions, use course context effectively, and verify important AI-generated answers.
---

Open [AI chat](/chat) to start a conversation. The assistant is most useful when your question includes a clear goal and enough course context.

## Ask an effective question

Use a specific question instead of a broad request.

```text
Explain the difference between precision and recall using a university admissions example. Then give me three questions to check my understanding.
```

For a document-based question, identify the course or source you want the assistant to use.

```text
Based on the Week 4 lecture notes, summarize the data-cleaning workflow as a numbered checklist.
```

## Continue the conversation

Ask follow-up questions when an explanation is too advanced, too short, or unclear. Useful follow-ups include:

- “Explain that in simpler language.”
- “Show each calculation step.”
- “Give me a different example.”
- “Quiz me without revealing the answer first.”

## Review every important answer

AI responses may contain mistakes or omit context. Verify academic rules, grades, dates, citations, safety information, and assessment requirements against official sources.

> Do not submit an AI-generated response as your own work when that would violate the course’s academic-integrity requirements.

## Protect sensitive information

Do not place passwords, identity documents, private student records, health information, or confidential university material in a chat. Share only the information needed for the learning task.
