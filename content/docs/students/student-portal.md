The student portal is the main workspace for learning activities. After signing in, open [My courses](/studentportal/mycourses) to begin.

![Student portal showing the course workspace and navigation](/docs/student-portal.png)

## My courses

The course list shows the classes connected to your account. Select a course to open its materials and learning tools. Course-specific pages may include resources, activities, and AI-supported features configured by the teacher.

If an expected course is missing, confirm that you are using the correct account and ask the course teacher to check enrollment.

## Calendar and reminders

Use the calendar to review scheduled items and the reminders area to keep track of upcoming work. These tools are organizational aids; always treat the official course syllabus or teacher announcement as the final source for deadlines.

## Study and document tools

- **AI Study Tools** helps you work with summaries, practice questions, and revision activities.
- **Documents RAG** connects questions with available course materials.
- **MFU Campus Guide** provides shortcuts to useful university resources.
- **MFU Social** lists selected campus communities and activities.

## Student settings

Open **Settings** to manage the options available for your profile and interface. Use the sign-out control when working on a shared computer.
