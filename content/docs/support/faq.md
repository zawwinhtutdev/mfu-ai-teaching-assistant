## Who can use the platform?

The platform is designed for MFU students, teachers, and authorized administrators. Registration requires an accepted university email address.

## Is the AI always correct?

No. AI-generated content can be incomplete or incorrect. Check important answers against course material, teacher instructions, and official university sources.

## Which browsers are supported?

Use a current version of Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge. Enable JavaScript and cookies for the site.

## Why is my course missing?

Confirm that you signed in with the correct account. If the course is still missing, ask the teacher or administrator to check your course access.

## Can students upload any document?

Available upload and document features depend on the student’s role, course, and current platform configuration. Never upload confidential or unauthorized material.

## Can teachers trust document-grounded answers automatically?

No. Grounding reduces unsupported answers but does not eliminate mistakes. Test the assistant with representative questions and compare answers with the source documents.

## Where can I ask for help?

Use [Troubleshooting](/docs/support/troubleshooting) first. If the problem continues, send the project team a message from the [contact page](/contact).
