---
title: Contact the team
description: Send a useful bug report, product question, or improvement suggestion to the MFU AI project team.
---

Open the [contact page](/contact) to send a message to the project team. Choose the feedback type that best describes the request.

## Before contacting support

Review [Troubleshooting](/docs/support/troubleshooting) and retry the action once in a current supported browser. Do not repeat an upload or administrative operation many times.

## Include useful details

Provide:

- Your role: student, teacher, or administrator.
- The page URL and feature name.
- The approximate time of the problem.
- The steps that led to it.
- The visible error message.
- What you expected to happen.

## Share screenshots safely

Crop the screenshot to the relevant area. Remove passwords, authentication links, private conversations, grades, student records, document contents, and unrelated browser tabs.

## Choose the correct feedback type

- **Bug Report:** something is broken or behaves unexpectedly.
- **Feature Request:** a new capability would help.
- **Improvement Suggestion:** an existing workflow could be clearer or faster.
- **General Feedback:** questions or comments that do not fit another category.

Never send passwords, API keys, or other secrets through the contact form.
